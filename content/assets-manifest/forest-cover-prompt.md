# Обложка «Загадочный лес»

Файл: `apps/mobile/assets/images/worlds/forest.png`.

Способ: встроенный инструмент `imagegen`, генерация и одна правка стиля. Референсы — предоставленные пользователем обложки `underwater.png` и `space.png`. Итоговый файл скопирован без дополнительной обработки.

## Исходный запрос

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: landscape raster cover for the children's language-learning game set 'Загадочный лес' (Mysterious Forest), ages 6–9. Generate one complete illustration, approximately 16:9, PNG.
Primary request: a friendly mysterious forest adventure in the same visual family as colorful children's underwater and outer-space game-cover illustrations: polished 2D cartoon artwork, rounded silhouettes, clean softly dark outlines, saturated candy colors, gentle gradient shading and glossy highlights, charming expressive animals, layered richly decorated environments. This is a finished game illustration, not a UI mockup.
Scene/backdrop: a magical sunlit emerald-and-turquoise woodland, rounded leafy trees framing an inviting winding golden path, soft shafts of sunlight, small flowers and mushrooms, a few glowing fireflies. Friendly and curious, never scary.
Subjects: the four animals taught in the actual set: an orange fox with white cheeks and a fluffy white-tipped tail, a friendly round brown bear, a small cream rabbit with long ears, and a cute wide-eyed owl perched on a low branch. Clear distinct silhouettes and happy natural expressions. Place their faces in the middle horizontal band so a top badge and a bottom title overlay will not cover them; keep all four visible in a modest center crop. Full scene with foliage continuing to all edges. Lower quarter is mostly quiet path and foliage for an app-rendered title.
Composition: wide, inviting, bright, playful and detailed like an illustrated kids adventure board-game box, readable as a small mobile card. Balance greens and turquoise with warm orange, sunny yellow and small coral accents. Soft volume within a flat cartoon illustration, not photorealism or 3D rendering.
Constraints: no lettering, no words, no logos, no watermarks, no frames, no interface controls. The Russian title and progress will be rendered separately by the application. No threatening animals, no human characters.
```

Remove realistic fur strokes, bark texture, painterly foliage, photographic light and depth of field. Do not use 3D/Pixar rendering. Keep animal faces legible in a small cover and in the central horizontal band. Bottom quarter is quiet path for a title overlay. Do not copy any text from the references: absolutely no text, letters, typography, frame, buttons or watermark. This is a finished forest cover PNG asset, not a UI mockup.
